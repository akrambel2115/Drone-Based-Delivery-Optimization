\## Required Work



\[cite\_start]Each group must: \[cite: 41]



1\. \[cite\_start]Students are required to propose two distinct mathematical formulations for the same problem. \[cite: 42]

&#x20;   \* \[cite\_start]The first formulation should follow a classical optimization approach (e.g., MILP-based assignment or scheduling model). \[cite: 43]

&#x20;   \* \[cite\_start]The second formulation must provide an alternative modeling perspective (e.g., graph-based, flow-based, or constraint programming formulation). \[cite: 44]

3\. \[cite\_start]Implement an exact method (Branch and Bound recommended). \[cite: 47]

4\. \[cite\_start]Develop two metaheuristics: \[cite: 48]

&#x20;   \* \[cite\_start]One population-based method (e.g., Genetic Algorithm) \[cite: 50]

6\. \[cite\_start]Perform an experimental study on at least 10 instances. \[cite: 52]
