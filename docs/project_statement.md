\## \[cite\_start]Introduction \[cite: 2]



\[cite\_start]With the rapid growth of e-commerce and advancements in autonomous technologies, drone-based delivery has become a promising solution for last-mile logistics\[cite: 3]. \[cite\_start]Drones can deliver packages quickly and efficiently, especially in congested urban areas\[cite: 4]. \[cite\_start]However, managing a fleet of drones raises several operational challenges, such as limited battery capacity, coordination between drones, and flight restrictions\[cite: 5]. \[cite\_start]These challenges make drone delivery an interesting and practical optimization problem\[cite: 6].



\## \[cite\_start]Problem Description \[cite: 7]



\[cite\_start]A logistics company operates a fleet of drones to deliver packages from a central depot to a set of customers\[cite: 8]. \[cite\_start]Each drone starts from the depot, delivers packages to assigned customers, and must return to the depot after completing its route\[cite: 9].



\## \[cite\_start]Objective \[cite: 10]



\[cite\_start]The goal is to design delivery routes for the drones such that all customers are served while minimizing the total energy consumption\[cite: 11].



\## \[cite\_start]Constraints \[cite: 12]



\[cite\_start]The solution must satisfy the following constraints\[cite: 13]:



\* \[cite\_start]Each customer must be served exactly once\[cite: 14].

\* \[cite\_start]Each drone starts and ends its route at the depot\[cite: 15].

\* \[cite\_start]Each drone has a limited battery capacity, which restricts the total distance or energy it can use during a route\[cite: 16].

\* \[cite\_start]Each drone has a limited payload capacity (maximum weight it can carry)\[cite: 17].

\* \[cite\_start]Routes must be feasible in terms of connectivity (a drone must follow a continuous path)\[cite: 18].

\* \[cite\_start]No-fly zones may exist, meaning that certain areas or paths are forbidden\[cite: 19].

\* \[cite\_start]Drones must avoid collisions, ensuring safe distances between simultaneous routes (optional advanced constraint)\[cite: 20].



\## \[cite\_start]Data and Benchmarks \[cite: 21]



\[cite\_start]Students may generate their own datasets by randomly creating customer locations, demands, and energy parameters\[cite: 22]. \[cite\_start]All assumptions regarding energy consumption and drone characteristics must be clearly stated and justified\[cite: 23].
